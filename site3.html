Sha'ab El Erg (Dolphin House)

A horseshoe-shaped offshore coral barrier where pods of spinner and Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins rest, nurture calves, and glide alongside certified freedivers in crystalline turquoise shallows.

Depth: 8m–15m · Protected Marine Biosphere
EXPEDITION SITE II HISTORIC REEF PASS

The Wrecks of Abu Nuhas

The famed underwater graveyard of the Red Sea shipping passage. Features intact Victorian steamships and cargo freighters encrusted in giant gorgonian sea fans, soft corals, and barracuda shoals.

Depth: 12m–32m · Advanced Diver Certification
CONSERVATION PROGRAM ESTABLISHED 1989

Red Sea Coral Nursery
